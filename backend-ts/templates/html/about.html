About Us

We're building the future
of development

Founded in 2024, we set out to create the tools we always wished existed. Our mission is to make software development faster, more enjoyable, and accessible to everyone.

Innovation First

We push boundaries and challenge conventions. Every feature we build is designed to be years ahead of the curve, setting new standards for developer tooling.

Developer Obsessed

Every decision starts with the developer experience. We dogfood our own products daily and iterate relentlessly based on real feedback from our community.

Open & Transparent

We build in public, share our roadmap openly, and believe that transparency creates trust. Our community shapes the product as much as we do.
